BRIEFING PROTOCOL

IMPORTANT RULES

Fair play is strictly enforced. Read and review all competition terms below before entering.

RULE 01 Mandatory Prior Registration
Teams and solo participants must register through the official registration form before their first ranked game of the new season. Matches played before confirmed registration will not count toward challenge milestones.
RULE 02 Permanent Format Lock
Once registered in Solo, Trio, or 5-Man format, switching formats mid-season is completely prohibited. If you register as a Trio, all ranked matches must be queued strictly as that registered Trio.
RULE 03 Strict Fixed Roster & No Substitutions
For Trio and 5-Man formats, the registered player roster cannot be changed after match 1. No standby players or substitutions are permitted. If one member cannot continue, the squad forfeits their progress.
RULE 04 Role Assignment Adherence
In Trio and 5-Man, players must stick to their registered primary role (Roamer, Mid, Jungle, Gold, Exp). In Solo, players can flex between their declared Primary and Backup roles when requested by draft lobbies.
RULE 05 Target Milestone: Mythic 15 Stars
The ₹500 team prize is unlocked only after reaching Mythic rank with a minimum of 15 stars earned (Mythic 15★) during the active event window. Reaching Mythic 0★ without 15 stars does not qualify.
RULE 06 MVP Evaluation (Winning Team Only)
The ₹300 MVP Champion award is calculated strictly using official Winning-Team MVP accolades. Losing-Team MVP (defeat) medals will be deducted or disregarded during total tally count.
RULE 07 Zero Account Sharing & Piloting
Every challenger must pilot their own personal account. If suspicious device ID changes, IP jumps, or unnatural hero pool shifts are detected, the entire squad is disqualified immediately.
RULE 08 Match Verification & History Transparency
In-game match history must remain set to 'Public' for the entirety of the challenge. Organizers reserve the right to audit replay files or match records at any checkpoint.
RULE 09 Sportsmanship & Code of Conduct
Deliberate feeding, win-trading, match fixing, toxic harassment towards fellow challengers or in public lobbies will cause forfeiture with no appeal.
RULE 10 Organizer Final Authority
In any unforeseen situation, tie-breakers, game client discrepancies, or rule interpretations, the decision of Rohan Btr is final and binding for all participants.
